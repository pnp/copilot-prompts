# Explore a Learner Note-Taking Product Idea


## Summary

This prompt helps non-technical product thinkers explore the learner note-taking problem before designing or building a solution.

## Prompt 💡

Purpose: Use this to make Copilot explore the learner note-taking problem before building.
I want to explore a product idea.
Problem:
Learners attend courses, workshops, and online sessions. They
take notes, save links, screenshots, prompts, and ideas, but later
their knowledge becomes scattered. They struggle to find, revise,
connect, and apply what they learned.
Help me understand this problem deeply.
Give me:
1. Who faces this problem?
2. What are the main pain points?
3. What are the symptoms?
4. What are the possible root causes?
5. What are the current workarounds?
6. Why existing note-taking apps may not fully solve this?
7. What would make a solution useful?
Keep it simple and practical as I am non-tech.

## Description ℹ️

The prompt guides Copilot through a practical problem-discovery exercise focused on how learners capture, organize, revisit, connect, and apply knowledge. It asks for affected user groups, pain points, symptoms, root causes, workarounds, limitations of existing apps, and qualities that would make a new solution useful.

## Instructions 📝

1. Navigate to [Microsoft Copilot](https://copilot.microsoft.com/) or open the Microsoft Copilot app.
2. Start a new conversation.
3. Paste the prompt into the conversation.
4. Review Copilot's analysis and use follow-up questions to explore the most relevant findings.

### Improvise Usage 🚀

Adapt the learner group, learning setting, or type of saved knowledge to explore a more specific audience. You can also ask Copilot to rank the pain points, turn root causes into assumptions to validate, or suggest interview questions for learner research.
