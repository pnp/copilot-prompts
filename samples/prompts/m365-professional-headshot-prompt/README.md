# Professional Headshot

![Screenshot of the professional headshot prompt in use](./assets/demo.jpeg)

## Summary

Create a cinematic professional headshot from an attached photo while preserving the person's facial features and customizing their attire.

## Prompt 💡

Cinematic professional headshot of this person, chest-up centered framing. Keep facial features exact. [Subject wears ...]. Background is a stylish, warm Microsoft office interior with soft natural wood accents and a hint of modern green foliage, beautifully blurred. Soft window light coming from the side. Confident, approachable expression with a friendly smile. Natural skin texture, subtle catchlight in both eyes, no excessive airbrushing. Shot on 35mm film look, shallow depth of field, sharp focus.

Replace [Subject wears...] with the attire you want. Examples:

Subject wears a high-quality, heavy-knit olive green mock-neck sweater, clean and modern.

Subject wears a structured, collarless dark gray asymmetrical zip jacket over a premium black crewneck t-shirt.

Subject wears a high-quality, heavy-knit olive green mock-neck sweater, clean and modern.

Subject wears a premium, perfectly fitted matte black t-shirt featuring a small, minimalist, sleek white Microsoft Copilot logo subtly printed on the left chest.

Subject wears a sleek, structured tailored dark blazer over a premium silk shirt with a minimalist gold chain necklace that subtly catches the light.

## Description ℹ️

This prompt transforms an attached portrait into a polished, cinematic headshot with warm office styling, natural lighting, realistic skin texture, and customizable professional attire.

## Instructions 📝

1. Open Microsoft Copilot chat.
2. Attach a clear photo of yourself.
3. Copy the prompt and replace `[Subject wears ...]` with your preferred attire.
4. Submit the prompt to generate your professional headshot.

### Improvise Usage 🚀

Customize the attire, office setting, lighting direction, expression, or photographic style to fit your professional profile and personal brand.
