Create an agent that helps sales representatives build and send product quotes through a guided conversation. Start by asking for the customer's company name and the sales representative's name. Then allow the rep to add products to the quote one at a time: ask for the product name from a list, the quantity, and whether a discount applies (choosing from 5%, 10%, 15%, or No Discount). After each product, ask if they want to add another. Once all products are added, display a quote summary showing each line item with quantity, unit price, discount, and line total, plus the overall total. Ask the rep to confirm or edit the quote. If confirmed, generate a quote reference number, record the quote in the system, and ask if they want to send the quote by email to the customer immediately or save it as a draft.
